WHAT IT IS
  A cozy recipe book for the family. Works on phone and PC, offline too.
  Online version (once published): https://arthur1134.github.io/recipes/

ADD RECIPES
  - 📷 Scan: take a photo of a recipe card/page (or pick one; add more pages if it
    continues). Tap "Read the recipe". The text is read ON YOUR PHONE (nothing uploaded,
    no paid service). Then "Sort into a recipe & review": it fills in the name,
    servings, times, ingredients and steps. Check it, fix anything, Save.
    The original scan picture stays with the recipe.
  - ＋ Add: type a recipe in.
  - Add food photos any time on the recipe page (Camera or Gallery). Photos are
    shrunk on the phone first (about 100–250 KB each).

GROCERY LIST
  On a recipe, tick ingredients → "Add checked to grocery list" (or "Add all").
  You can shorten names and pick the grocery section before adding. Items already
  on the list are not added twice; items in the Grocery List catalogue (Onions,
  Eggs, Chicken Thighs...) are just ticked. Salt/pepper/water start un-ticked.
  Only "Parent" members (you and your wife) see these buttons.

SYNC + SHARING (online version only)
  - "Sign in with Google" at the top. Recipes are saved in your Google Drive folder
    RecipeBook (recipe-book.json + 8 recipe-photos-N.json files).
  - More ☰ → "Share with a parent" (wife) or "Share with a kid". Type their Google
    e-mail. You get a link to send them (Google does not e-mail them).
  - They open the link, tap "Open the family recipe book", sign in with Google,
    select ALL the files shown, press Select. Done once per phone.
  - Kids can view, add and edit recipes. They do not get grocery buttons.
  - While the Google project is in "Testing" mode, every person's Google account must
    be added as a Test user in Google Cloud (see below), and Google asks for
    sign-in again about once a week.

INSTALL ON A PHONE
  iPhone: open the link in Safari → Share → "Add to Home Screen".
  Android: open it in Chrome → ⋮ → "Add to Home screen" / "Install app".

PC COPY
  Double-clicking index.html works for reading/typing recipes on that PC, but
  scanning and Google sync need the online version (browsers block them for files).

BACKUP
  More ☰ → Export (with photos) / Import backup. Import combines, never deletes.
