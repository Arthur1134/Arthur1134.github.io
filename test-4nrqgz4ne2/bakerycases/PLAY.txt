HOW TO PLAY BAKERY CASES  (Retro Gaming Dad)
- Tap a stand, then another: the top run of matching sweets moves if the target is empty or has the same flavor on top, with room (4 per stand).
- Win: every stand empty or holding 4 of one flavor ("SOLD").
- TURN STANDS (purple ↻, from Case 4): tap TURN, then the stand - its stack flips upside-down. Limited turns per case; some cases (11+) need a turn.
- CLOSED stands (from Case 7) open once you sell out any flavor.
- COOLING ❄ (from Case 11): chilled sweets can't move or be stacked on; move sweets in/out of a neighbouring stand to thaw them.
- Undo, Hint, Restart. 3 stars at or under par, 2 stars within 35%.
- Daily Special on the title screen: one new case per day.
- Bakery Radio (Settings): 5 original WebAudio tracks - Morning Bake, Sugar Rush (start), Window Display (8 cases), Sprinkle Disco (20), Golden Crust (all 44). Separate SFX and music volume.
