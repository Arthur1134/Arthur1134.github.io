How to Play

🎞 Tap a deck tray, then another, to slide the top run of same-color cassettes across. It only fits if the target is empty or its top tape is the same color, and there is room (4 per tray).

🏆 Win when every tray is empty or holds 4 tapes of one color.

🔄 Side A/B: pink A/B trays can be flipped. Tap FLIP, then the tray: the stack turns over (top ↔ bottom). Flips are limited per level and cost a move.

🔒 Locked trays open when you complete any color.

❄ Iced tapes can't move or be stacked on. Pour into or out of a neighbouring tray to thaw them.

⭐ Finish at or under par for 3 stars.
