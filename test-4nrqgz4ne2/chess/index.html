How to Play

Choose vs Player for two-player hotseat or vs Computer for an AI opponent. Pick Easy / Medium / Hard and whether you play White or Black. Optional 5 or 10 minute chess clocks. Your game is saved automatically and restored when you reopen the page.

Chess: Standard rules including castling, en passant and promotion (choose your piece). Checkmate wins; stalemate, threefold repetition, the 50-move rule and insufficient material are draws. Running out of time loses (draw if the opponent cannot mate).

Checkers: English draughts on dark squares. Captures are mandatory. Multi-jumps continue with the same piece until no more captures; reaching the far row crowns a king and ends the turn. Kings move and capture in all diagonal directions. Capture all opponent pieces or leave them with no moves to win. 40 moves each without a capture or man move, or threefold repetition, is a draw. Squares are numbered 1–32 for notation.
