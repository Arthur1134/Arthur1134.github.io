Night-city scooter delivery

v1.0.0 · Retro Gaming Dad

HOW TO PLAY

Your scooter dashes forward on its own. Steer to dodge traffic, barriers and potholes.

Ride over 📦 packages to grab them (carry up to 3).

FLING them left or right into the glowing drop zones on the sidewalk as you pass.

Deliveries add time. Chain them fast for a COMBO — bigger combos stretch the clock more!

⏱ clocks add time · ⚡ pads give a speed burst · oil makes you skid · 3 crashes = wrecked.

Keys: A/D or ←/→ steer · W/↑ boost · S/↓ brake · Q/J fling left · E/K fling right · Space auto-fling · P/Esc pause

Touch: drag to steer · buttons to fling & boost
