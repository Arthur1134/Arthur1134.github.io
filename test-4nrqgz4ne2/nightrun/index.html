About

Night Run is an original top-down road-chase arcade game. Avoid Street Ghost civilian traffic, take down Gridlock Raiders, watch for Sky Wasp drones, and drive into a Supply Hauler to restock ammo. Weapons: front gun, side ram (steer into hostiles), short boost. Score from distance and hostile takedowns. Three lives.
