Keyboard

Controls

Click an action, then press a key. Two actions never share a key. Esc cancels a rebind. Controller: D-pad or left stick moves, A places the highlighted number, bumpers change that number, B erases, X toggles notes, Y undoes, LT gives a hint, RT toggles mistake checking, Start pauses. In menus, A selects and B goes back.
